## 1. Purpose and Policy

Per Decision **D3**, every previously open question across Project Aegis documentation has been classified as either:
- **Decided:** An assumed baseline value is adopted with documented engineering rationale to allow system construction to proceed without blocking.
- **Deferred:** The decision is postponed until a specific external trigger occurs, with an explicit named owner.

Every decision that relies on customer interview validation or legal review is also mirrored as an assumption in [assumptions-register.md](file:///c:/dev/aegis/docs/00-decisions/assumptions-register.md) subject to **Gate G1** (pre-customer data entry) or **Gate G2** (pre-paid contract).
